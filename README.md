# Minamo

A free, lightweight anime discovery and tracking app by **Aoki Yoshi**.
Find what to watch, see what airs today, and keep track of everything you watch. All on your own device.

**Try it online:** https://theaokiyoshi.github.io/Minamo/

## Features

- **Discover**: trending, popular, new and upcoming anime
- **Schedule**: a monthly calendar of what airs each day
- **Search**: 134 tags and detailed filters (genres, format, status, season, year, rating and more)
- **Bookmarks**: Watching, Planning, Completed, On hold and Dropped, with episode tracking and notes
- **Details**: synopsis, rating, episodes, characters, staff, related anime and where to watch
- **Your style**: four themes, accent colours, four fonts and three corner styles
- **Backup**: export and import your lists and settings

## Install

**Android:** download the latest `Minamo-V1.0.apk` from the [Releases](https://github.com/theaokiyoshi/Minamo/releases) page, open it and allow the install when Android asks.

**Any device:** open the link above in your browser. You can add it to your home screen from the browser menu.

An internet connection is needed to load anime data.

## Privacy

There are no accounts and no ads. Your bookmarks, notes and settings are stored only on your device. Use Settings > Data to back them up.
